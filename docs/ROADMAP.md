# Roadmap

## Near Term

- Email-to-ticket intake with verified sender mapping, attachment quarantine, and manager confirmation
- Shared distributed rate limiting, structured production logging, metrics, alerting, and outbox queue dashboards
- Object storage with signed delivery, malware scanning, orphan cleanup, retention jobs, and attachment thumbnails
- MFA/SSO, richer member lifecycle controls, accessibility audit, and browser coverage across Chromium/WebKit/mobile devices

## Product Expansion

- Explicit multi-company isolation with tenant-scoped keys, policies, migrations, and administration
- Calendar integration for manager, tenant, and contractor availability with webhook reconciliation
- Contractor directory, licensing/insurance records, availability windows, and service performance reporting
- Approved templates for email intake, status communications, and multilingual tenant messages

## Later Exploration

- Preventive maintenance schedules linked to assets and compliance evidence
- Human-reviewed recurring-issue signals and portfolio trend analysis
- Native mobile experiences only after responsive workflow usage justifies them
- Payments, invoicing, or accounting integration only with dedicated financial controls and audit design

Automatic diagnosis, autonomous urgency decisions, and autonomous assignment/approval are not planned without a separate safety and accountability review.
